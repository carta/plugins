# Carta Cap Table Field Definitions Glossary

## Cap Table Report

| Field | Type | Tabs | Definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Acceleration | Yes/No | Share classes, Equity Incentive Plan | Indicates whether the security is subject to vesting acceleration, meaning that some or all unvested shares may vest early under specific conditions (e.g., company acquisition, termination without cause). Displays "Yes" if acceleration applies and "No" if it does not. |
| Acceleration Text | Custom text | Share classes, Equity Incentive Plan | A description of conditions under which the vesting schedule may be accelerated, allowing shares to vest earlier than originally planned. |
| Address | Street Address (e.g., 111 Main St) | Share classes | The street address of the stakeholder, excluding city, state, and other location details. |
| Amendment | Repricing | Equity Incentive Plan | Indicates whether the option grant is the product of a platform-processed repricing. This field returns "Repricing" only if the grant was issued from a repricing through the platform. Modifications made during issuance that change the exercise price do not trigger this field. |
| Amendment Details | Repricing details (e.g., 6/20/2024 from ES-40 at $3.80) | Equity Incentive Plan | Displays the details of a formal repricing event for the option grant, including the effective date, original security ID, and new exercise price. This field remains blank if no platform-processed repricing has occurred. |
| Balance Shares Reissued | Number of shares | Share classes | The number of shares that remain with the original stakeholder after a transaction, such as a partial repurchase or partial transfer. This reflects the portion of shares not transferred or repurchased, ensuring that only the remaining balance is recorded under this field. |
| Base Award Type | ISO, NSO, INTL, RSU, RSA, CBU, SAR etc. | Equity Incentive Plan | The original classification of the award based on its tax treatment or structure at issuance. Note that if a security was granted as an ISO, this field will remain ISO even if the award is later disqualified due to termination or other reasons. |
| Board Approval Date | Date | Share classes, Equity Incentive Plan, Warrant ledger, Convertible ledger | The date when the security was approved by the board of directors. |
| Canceled Date | Date | Share classes, Equity Incentive Plan, Warrant ledger, Convertible ledger | The date when the security was canceled by the issuer. |
| Cancellation Reason | Acquisition, Conversion, Dissolved, Merger, Public offering, Recapitalization, Secondary, Spinoff, Swap or Write off cancel | Share classes, Equity Incentive Plan, Warrant ledger, Convertible ledger | The recorded reason for the security’s cancellation. |
| Cash Contributed to Company | Monetary value | Share classes | The amount of cash received by the company in exchange for the security. This reflects primary transactions where the company directly issued shares for payment. In secondary transactions, where shares are sold between stakeholders without company involvement, this field will be empty. To see the total amount paid by the stakeholder, refer to Cash Paid in the Securities Ledger report. |
| Cash Raised | Monetary value | Summary | The total gross amount of new capital the company has raised through the issuance of new shares. This sum reflects the cash received from original share issuances within a specific share class or security type, excluding proceeds from secondary transactions or warrant exercises. |
| Change in Control Percent | Percentage | Convertible ledger | The ownership percentage threshold that, if met during a company acquisition or sale, may trigger special terms for the noteholders, such as automatic repayment or conversion. |
| City | City (e.g., Mountain View) | Share classes | The city associated with the stakeholder’s address. |
| Conversion Discount | Percentage | Convertible ledger | The percentage discount applied to the price per share when the note converts into equity, providing noteholders with a lower purchase price compared to new investors. |
| Conversion Trigger | Monetary value | Convertible ledger | The minimum company valuation required in a qualifying equity financing round to trigger the automatic conversion of the convertible note into equity. This amount sets the threshold at which noteholders’ investment converts rather than remaining as debt. |
| Converted Date | Date | Convertible ledger | The date when the convertible security was converted into equity. This marks the point at which the note or SAFE transitioned from debt (or a convertible instrument) into shares based on its conversion terms. If no conversion has occurred, this field remains blank. |
| Cost Center | Custom text | Share classes, Equity Incentive Plan, Warrant ledger, Convertible ledger | The internal accounting designation assigned to the stakeholder, used for tracking expenses and financial reporting. |
| Country | Country (e.g., USA) | Share classes | The country associated with the stakeholder’s address. |
| Date Fully Vested | Date | Equity Incentive Plan | The date when the security completes its vesting schedule, meaning all shares have fully vested based on the applicable vesting conditions. If the vesting schedule includes both time-based and performance-based criteria, this date reflects when both conditions have been met. |
| Death Exercise Period | Custom period (e.g., 3 Months, 1 Year, 0 Days) | Equity Incentive Plan | The period during which a stakeholder’s vested options may be exercised by their beneficiaries or estate following the stakeholder’s passing. |
| Debt Canceled | Monetary value | Share classes | The monetary value of the debt that the issuer owed to the stakeholder and was canceled in exchange for the security. |
| Destination | Unique Security ID | Share classes, Equity Incentive Plan, Warrant ledger, Convertible ledger | The security that follows in the transaction history, linked through an event such as exercise, settlement, repricing, conversion, transfer, or repurchase. |
| Disability Exercise Period | Custom period (e.g., 3 Months, 1 Year, 0 Days) | Equity Incentive Plan | The period during which a stakeholder can exercise their vested options if they become permanently disabled, as defined by the company’s equity plan. |
| Dividend Ignoring Cancellation | Monetary value | Share classes | The total dividends associated with the security, calculated without adjusting for any cancellations or security terminations. This field reflects the dividends that would have been accrued or distributed based on the original terms of the security, regardless of whether the security was later canceled or forfeited |
| Employee ID | Custom Employee ID | Share classes, Equity Incentive Plan | A unique identifier assigned to the stakeholder by the company for employment or administrative tracking purposes. |
| Exercise Dates | Exercise details (e.g., 10/25/2017 (1,200 shares) - ACH) | Equity Incentive Plan | A record of all exercise transactions for the security, including the date of exercise, number of shares exercised, and payment method. Transactions are listed chronologically and may indicate different methods such as manual entry, ACH, or wire transfer. |
| Exercise Dates | Exercise details (e.g., 10/25/2017 (1,200 shares) - manually) | Warrant ledger | A record of all exercise transactions for the security, including the date of exercise, number of shares exercised, and payment method. Transactions are listed chronologically. |
| Exercise Price | Monetary value | Equity Incentive Plan, Warrant ledger | The fixed price per share that the stakeholder must pay to exercise the option and purchase the underlying security. |
| Exercised Shares | Number of shares | Warrant ledger | The number of shares the stakeholder has purchased by exercising their right to acquire the underlying shares of a security. |
| Federal Exemption | Rule 701, Section 4(a)(2), Section 4(a)(1-1/2), Section 4(a)(7), Rule 144, Reg D - 506(b), Reg D - 506(c), Reg D - 506, Reg D - 505, Reg D 504, Reg S, Reg A (Tier 1), Reg A (Tier 2), Reg CF, Non-U.S., Other | Share classes, Equity Incentive Plan, Warrant ledger, Convertible ledger | The U.S. securities law exemption under which the security was issued, allowing the company to grant or sell securities without SEC registration. The applicable exemption depends on factors such as the type of offering, investor eligibility, and disclosure requirements. |
| Formatted Security ID | Unique Security ID | Share classes, Equity Incentive Plan, Warrant ledger, Convertible ledger | Unique security identifier, formatted with leading zeroes to facilitate filtering and sorting. |
| Full Address | Full Address (e.g., 111 Main St, Mountain View, CA, 94114, USA) | Share classes | The stakeholder’s complete mailing address, including street address, city, state, zip code, and country. |
| Fully Diluted Ownership | Percentage | Summary | The percentage of company ownership a stakeholder holds when accounting for all fully diluted shares. This metric considers outstanding stock as well as potential dilution from options, warrants, RSUs, and equity plan reserves, providing a more comprehensive view of ownership distribution. |
| Fully Diluted Ownership | Percentage | Intermediate, Detailed | The percentage of company ownership the stakeholder holds when accounting for all fully diluted shares. This metric considers outstanding stock as well as potential dilution from options, warrants, RSUs, and equity plan reserves, providing a more comprehensive view of ownership distribution. |
| Fully Diluted Shares | Number of shares | Summary | The total number of shares assuming all convertible securities are exercised or converted into common stock. This count includes outstanding shares plus options, warrants, RSUs, SARs, and any remaining available shares under an equity plan. |
| Fully Diluted Shares | Number of shares | Intermediate, Detailed | The total number of shares the stakeholder owns or could own if all their convertible securities were exercised or converted into common stock. This includes currently outstanding shares plus any options, warrants, RSUs, SARs, or other convertible instruments the stakeholder holds that could be exercised or settled. |
| Grant Life Expiration Date | Date | Equity Incentive Plan, Warrant ledger | The date when the security’s contractual term ends, after which it can no longer be exercised or remains valid. |
| Interest | Monetary value | Convertible ledger | The total amount of interest accrued on the convertible note over time, based on the agreed-upon interest rate and accrual method. |
| Interest Rate | Percentage | Convertible ledger | The annual percentage rate applied to the convertible note to determine the amount of accrued interest. |
| Involuntary Termination Exercise Period | Custom period (e.g., 3 Months, 1 Year, 0 Days) | Equity Incentive Plan | The period during which a stakeholder can exercise their vested options after being terminated by the company without cause (e.g., layoffs, position elimination). |
| Is Early Exercisable | Yes/No | Equity Incentive Plan | Indicates whether the option can be exercised before the vesting conditions have been met, subject to applicable plan rules and restrictions. |
| Is ISO Disqualified? | Yes/No | Equity Incentive Plan | Indicates whether the ISO has lost its tax-qualified status due to disqualification events such as expiration of the post-termination exercise period (PTEP), or modification of the grant terms. Returns "Yes" if the ISO is disqualified and "No" if it retains its preferential tax treatment. |
| Is ISO/NSO Split? | Yes/No | Equity Incentive Plan | Indicates whether the option has been split into ISO and NSO portions due to exceeding the $100K ISO limit, as defined under IRC Section 422. |
| ISO Disqualification Date | Date | Equity Incentive Plan | The date when an option lost its ISO tax-qualified status. This can occur automatically due to the Post-Termination Exercise Period (PTEP) expiration or manually due to a security modification that disqualifies the ISO treatment. |
| Issue Date | Date | Share classes, Equity Incentive Plan, Warrant ledger, Convertible ledger | The date when the security was officially issued to the stakeholder, marking the start of ownership or rights associated with it. |
| Job Title | Custom text | Share classes, Equity Incentive Plan, Warrant ledger, Convertible ledger | The stakeholder’s role or position within the company, if applicable. |
| Last Day to Exercise | Date | Equity Incentive Plan | The final date when a terminated stakeholder can exercise vested options before the post-termination exercise period ends. After this date, the right to purchase shares expires. |
| Last Day to Repurchase | Date | Share classes | The last date the issuer has the right to repurchase shares from the stakeholder under the terms of the equity plan. |
| Legend | Custom text | Share classes | A restriction or disclaimer placed on a security to indicate specific transfer limitations, regulatory requirements, or other conditions affecting its sale or ownership. Legends are typically used to comply with securities laws and may require removal before shares can be freely transferred. |
| Liquidation Preferences | Liquidation preferences (e.g., Converts 1:1 CSE, 6% cumulative non-cash dividend, 1x multiple; Converts 1:1 CSE, 2x participating preferred cap, 8% non-cumulative non-cash dividend, 1x multiple) | Share classes | The terms defining how a preferred security converts and its priority in a liquidity event (e.g., acquisition, IPO, or company dissolution). This field outlines key details such as the conversion ratio to common stock (CSE), participation rights, dividend terms, and liquidation multiple. It specifies whether dividends are cumulative or non-cumulative, whether the security has a participating preferred cap, and the multiple of the original investment that must be paid before common shareholders receive proceeds. For common share classes, this field returns "N/A" as they do not have liquidation preferences. |
| Maturity Date | Date | Convertible ledger | The date when the convertible note reaches its due date, at which point it must be repaid, converted into equity, or renegotiated. |
| Name | Custom text | Intermediate, Detailed | Name of the stakeholder. |
| Non-Returning Shares | Number of shares | Share classes | The number of shares that were canceled and do not return to the equity pool for future issuance. |
| Note Block Name | Custom text | Convertible ledger | The name assigned to a group of convertible securities, such as SAFEs or convertible notes, that share the same or similar terms. This grouping allows issuers to organize convertibles based on characteristics like valuation cap, discount rate, and governing document before issuing them. |
| Notes | Custom text | Share classes, Equity Incentive Plan, Warrant ledger, Convertible ledger | Additional information or comments related to the security or stakeholder, entered during issuance or updated later for record-keeping. This field may include context on transactions, special conditions, or internal remarks. |
| Origin | Unique Security ID | Share classes, Equity Incentive Plan | The previous security in the transaction history, linked through events such as exercise, settlement, repricing, conversion, transfer, or repurchase. |
| Origin | Unique Security ID | Convertible ledger | The previous security in the transaction history, linked through events such as transfer. |
| Original Acquisition Date | Date | Share classes | The date when the original security in the transaction history was first acquired, before any transfers, exercises, or conversions. |
| Other Consideration | Monetary value | Share classes, Convertible ledger | The monetary value of any additional non-cash compensation the stakeholder provided in exchange for the security, excluding cash, debt cancellation, or transferred intellectual property. |
| Outstanding Ownership | Percentage | Intermediate, Detailed | The percentage of company ownership the stakeholder holds based only on outstanding shares. This metric reflects the stakeholder’s ownership of the company without accounting for potential dilution from unexercised or unconverted securities. |
| Outstanding Shares | Number of shares | Intermediate, Detailed | The total number of shares that have been issued and are currently held by the stakeholder. This includes only stock that is currently outstanding and does not include options, warrants, RSUs, SARs, or other convertible securities that have not yet been exercised or settled. |
| Outstanding Time Unvested Quantity | Number of shares | Equity Incentive Plan | The total number of shares that have not yet vested based on time-based conditions and remain outstanding. If the vesting schedule includes both time-based and performance-based criteria, this count reflects only the shares that are subject to time-based vesting, regardless of performance-based conditions. |
| Outstanding Time Vested Quantity | Number of shares | Equity Incentive Plan | The total number of shares that have vested based on time-based conditions and remain outstanding. If the vesting schedule includes both time-based and performance-based criteria, this count reflects only the shares that have met the time-based requirement, regardless of performance-based conditions. |
| Outstanding Unvested Quantity | Number of shares | Share classes, Equity Incentive Plan | The total number of shares that have not yet fully vested (because they have not met all applicable vesting conditions, whether time-based, performance-based, or both) and remain outstanding. |
| Outstanding Vested Quantity | Number of shares | Share classes, Equity Incentive Plan | The total number of shares that have fully vested (meeting all applicable vesting conditions, whether time-based, performance-based, or both) and remain outstanding. |
| Paper Status | Yes/No | Share classes, Equity Incentive Plan | Indicates whether the security was issued as a paper certificate outside the platform. This may apply to securities created during onboarding but not yet accepted by the stakeholder. |
| Paper Status | Yes/No | Warrant ledger, Convertible ledger | Indicates whether the security was issued as a paper security outside the platform. This may apply to securities created during onboarding but not yet accepted by the stakeholder. |
| Plan Name | Custom text | Share classes, Equity Incentive Plan | Name of the equity plan under which the security was granted or issued. |
| Price Paid Per Share | Monetary value | Share classes | The effective cost per share that the stakeholder incurred to acquire the security. Calculated as the total sum of Cash Paid, Debt Canceled, Value of IP Transferred, and Other Consideration, divided by the total number of shares acquired. |
| Principal | Monetary value | Convertible ledger | The original amount invested in the convertible security, excluding any accrued interest or additional consideration. This represents the face value of the note or SAFE at issuance, which may later convert into equity or be repaid according to its terms. |
| Purchase Price | Monetary value | Warrant ledger | The amount of cash paid to acquire the warrant at issuance, not the amount paid to exercise it into a certificate. This reflects the initial cost of obtaining the warrant itself, separate from any future exercise price required to convert it into shares. |
| Quantity Canceled | Number of shares | Share classes, Equity Incentive Plan, Warrant ledger | The number of shares that are no longer outstanding, typically due to contract termination, expiration, or clerical correction. |
| Quantity Converted | Number of shares | Share classes | The number of shares converted from one class or security type to another. |
| Quantity Exercised | Number of shares | Equity Incentive Plan | The number of shares the stakeholder has purchased by exercising their right to acquire the underlying shares of a security. |
| Quantity Expired | Number of shares | Equity Incentive Plan, Warrant ledger | The number of vested shares that were returned to the issuer because the security’s exercise or settlement window expired, either due to termination or reaching the end of its lifetime. |
| Quantity Forfeited | Number of shares | Equity Incentive Plan | The number of shares that did not vest and were returned to the issuer due to termination or failure to meet vesting conditions. |
| Quantity Issued | Number of shares | Share classes, Equity Incentive Plan, Warrant ledger | Total number of shares or units issued, including any additional quantity reserved at issuance to account for performance conditions with a maximum payout other than 100%. |
| Quantity Outstanding | Number of shares | Share classes, Equity Incentive Plan, Warrant ledger | Total number of shares or units that remain active and have not been exercised, forfeited, or otherwise removed from circulation. |
| Quantity Repurchased | Number of shares | Share classes | The number of shares repurchased from the stakeholder and returned to the issuer. |
| Quantity Transferred | Number of shares | Share classes, Warrant ledger | The number of shares transferred from one stakeholder to another. |
| Relationship | Advisor, Ex-Advisor, Board member, Ex-Board member, Consultant, Ex-Consultant, Employee, Ex-Employee, Executive, Founder, Ex-Founder, International Employee, Ex-International Employee, Investor, Officer, Other | Share classes, Equity Incentive Plan, Warrant ledger, Convertible ledger | The stakeholder’s connection  or affiliation with the company. |
| Repurchase Date | Date | Share classes | The date when the issuer repurchased shares from the stakeholder. |
| Retirement Exercise Period | Custom period (e.g., 3 Months, 1 Year, 0 Days) | Equity Incentive Plan | The period during which a stakeholder can exercise their vested options after retiring, subject to the company’s retirement policy and plan terms. |
| Rule 144 Date | Date | Share classes | The date that marks the start of the SEC Rule 144 holding period. This date is used to determine when the shares may become eligible for resale under Rule 144, should the company go public. If the security is transferred, the holding period may reset based on the new holder’s acquisition date. |
| Security ID | Unique Security ID (truncated) | Share classes, Equity Incentive Plan, Warrant ledger, Convertible ledger | Unique security identifier, recorded as-is in the platform. |
| Share Class | Custom text, though preferred classes must end with "Preferred" | Share classes, Warrant ledger | Name of the share class associated with the security (e.g., Common, Series Seed Preferred). |
| Shares Authorized | Number of shares | Summary | The maximum number of shares a company is legally permitted to issue, as outlined in its incorporation documents or equity plan. This count includes shares that may be issued in the future but does not assume all authorized shares will be allocated. |
| Shares Issued and Outstanding | Number of shares | Summary | The total number of shares that have been issued and are currently held by stakeholders. This includes shares issued to founders, employees, investors, and other stakeholders but excludes options, warrants, RSUs, and other convertible securities that have not yet been exercised or settled. |
| Source | Source text (e.g., Exercised ISO option, ES-1; Converted from note CN-4) | Share classes | This field tracks whether the security was newly issued, issued as an RSA, transferred between stakeholders, exercised from an option, converted from a note, or repurchased. The entry may include references to specific transaction IDs, such as option grants or convertible notes. |
| Stakeholder Email | Email address | Share classes, Equity Incentive Plan, Warrant ledger, Convertible ledger | Email address of the stakeholder associated with the security. |
| Stakeholder ID | Unique Stakeholder ID | Intermediate, Detailed, Share classes, Equity Incentive Plan, Warrant ledger, Convertible ledger | A unique identifier assigned to the stakeholder within the platform for record-keeping and reference. |
| Stakeholder Name | Custom text | Share classes, Equity Incentive Plan, Warrant ledger, Convertible ledger | Name of the stakeholder associated with the security. |
| State | State or Province (e.g., CA) | Share classes | The state or province associated with the stakeholder’s address. |
| State Exemption | Custom text | Share classes, Equity Incentive Plan, Warrant ledger, Convertible ledger | The state-level securities law exemption under which the security was issued, allowing the company to offer or sell securities without state registration. This exemption varies by jurisdiction and is determined based on the company’s location, the investor’s residency, and applicable state regulations. |
| State of Residency | Two-letter U.S. state code (e.g., CA, NY) or three-letter country code (e.g., CAN, GBR) | Share classes, Equity Incentive Plan, Warrant ledger, Convertible ledger | The stakeholder’s state of residence at the time of security issuance. This field is used for blue-sky compliance filings and may be referenced for board approval of equity awards. Unlike the State field in the stakeholder’s address, which is tied to their profile and updated as needed, State of Residency is recorded at the security level and remains fixed based on the issuance details |
| Termination Date | Date | Share classes, Warrant ledger | The date when a stakeholder’s employment or contractual relationship with the company officially ends, serving as the cutoff for time-based vesting of their securities. For non-employees, this represents the end of their contractual agreement, which similarly stops vesting. |
| Termination Date | Date | Equity Incentive Plan | The date when a stakeholder’s employment or contractual relationship with the company officially ends, serving as the cutoff for time-based vesting of their securities. If the security has a post-termination exercise period (PTEP), this date also marks the start of that period. For non-employees, this represents the end of their contractual agreement, which similarly stops vesting. |
| Termination Date | Date | Convertible ledger | The date when a stakeholder’s employment or contractual relationship with the company officially ends, serving as the cutoff for time-based vesting of their securities.For non-employees, this represents the end of their contractual agreement, which similarly stops vesting. |
| Termination with Cause Exercise Period | Custom period (e.g., 3 Months, 1 Year, 0 Days) | Equity Incentive Plan | The period during which a stakeholder can exercise their vested options after being terminated for cause, which may include misconduct, policy violations, or other breaches of contract. This period is often shorter or nonexistent. |
| Total | Monetary value | Convertible ledger | The sum of the principal and interest accrued on the convertible security. This represents the full outstanding amount that may be repaid or converted into equity, reflecting both the original investment and any accumulated interest over time. |
| Total Time Vested Quantity | Number of shares | Equity Incentive Plan | The total number of shares that have vested based on time-based conditions, regardless of whether they remain outstanding. If the vesting schedule includes both time-based and performance-based criteria, this count reflects only the shares that have met the time-based requirement, independent of performance conditions |
| Total Vested Quantity | Number of shares | Equity Incentive Plan | The total number of shares that have fully vested. If the vesting schedule includes both time-based and performance-based conditions, shares are considered vested only when both criteria have been met. This count includes vested shares regardless of whether they are still outstanding. |
| Valuation Cap | Monetary value | Convertible ledger | The maximum company valuation at which the note converts into equity, ensuring noteholders receive a more favorable conversion price in the event of a future financing round. |
| Value of IP Transferred | Monetary value | Share classes | The monetary value assigned to intellectual property (IP) that the stakeholder transferred to the issuer as consideration for the security. |
| Vesting Schedule | Custom text | Share classes, Equity Incentive Plan | A description of how and when the award vests over time, detailing the timing and structure of vesting (e.g., monthly vesting, cliff period). |
| Vesting Start Date | Date | Share classes, Equity Incentive Plan | The date from which the vesting schedule begins, marking the start of the vesting period for the award. |
| Voluntary Termination Exercise Period | Custom period (e.g., 3 Months, 1 Year, 0 Days) | Equity Incentive Plan | The period during which a stakeholder can exercise their vested options after voluntarily leaving the company (e.g., resignation). |
| Zip Code | Postal code (e.g., 94114) | Share classes | The postal code associated with the stakeholder’s address. |
